About Us

At our Mobile Service Center, we specialize in breathing life back into your damaged devices. With expert technicians, state-of-the-art repair tools, and a commitment to using the highest quality parts, we ensure your smartphone functions flawlessly. From shattered screens to complex motherboard repairs, we handle it all with precision and care.

Things to Note Before Servicing


  • Data Backup: Please ensure you back up all personal data before submitting your device. We are not responsible for data loss during hardware repairs.
  • Diagnostic Fee: A nominal diagnostic fee may apply if you choose not to proceed with the repair after we identify the issue.
  • Warranty Terms: We provide a 90-day warranty strictly on the replacement parts used. This does not cover physical or liquid damage post-repair.
  • Water Damage: Devices with liquid damage carry no guarantees, as corrosion can cause secondary issues over time.
  • Privacy Guaranteed: Your privacy is our priority. We strictly prohibit our technicians from accessing your personal data.
